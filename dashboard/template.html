Which menu changes make a restaurant more profit, and how sure can we be?

A 30-item bistro, simulated. Change an ingredient cost or a price and see what happens to contribution profit, the menu engineering matrix, and the experiments worth running next.

Elasticity is an assumption here, not a measurement. Every number that depends on it is hatched, and shown as a range across a low, medium and high band you can edit. The ranked experiment backlog is how you would measure it.
